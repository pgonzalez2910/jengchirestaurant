DATABASE — Weekly Inventory Counts

The weekly inventory database starts on 07/12/2026. Each Sunday column is the ending physical inventory count in individual dumpling pieces—not sales and not weekly usage. Entered cells save automatically and remain light green after saving. Inventory may increase after production. Enter 0 for a verified zero; a blank cell means the count is missing.

Workbook Preview
Inventory snapshots are saved permanently by count date. The website never subtracts one inventory count from another to call it sales. Prep decisions use the selected physical count and the separate rolling sales-based par. A final Prep Sheet is withheld until all 21 products are counted.
